WHY WE DO WHAT WE DO

It’s more than paint.
It’s how you feel.

A car is part of everyday life — the school run, the road trip, the long way home. When it needs a little care, it deserves a team that pays attention.

At Odamblason, we bring together the hands-on work of painting, panel beating and car care. We take time to understand what your vehicle needs, then focus on doing the job with care and pride.

We’re based in Zimmerman, ready to welcome drivers from across Nairobi. Come by, give us a call, and let’s talk through what you have in mind.
